Styling Guide

What to Wear to Your
Family Portraits Session

✦ Flowy & Romantic

Flowy Gowns

Flowing fabric flatters beautifully, emphasising the bump. Choose earthy tones, dusty rose, sage, or ivory. My client closet has 30+ gowns available free of charge.

✦ Modern & Minimal

Form-Fitting Looks

A fitted bodysuit or wrap dress beautifully showcases the bump in its full glory. Neutral tones and soft textures photograph timlessly and age-proof in 20 years.

✦ Partner & Family

Coordinating Outfits

Partners and siblings look best in complementary (not matching) tones. Avoid logos and busy prints. Think: linen, neutrals, soft denim, and classic white shirts.

🎨

Colour Palette

Earthy tones, blush, sage, ivory, and warm whites photograph beautifully. Avoid neon or very dark colours which can cast colour on skin.

💅

Hair & Makeup

Professional hair & makeup is recommended. I can connect you with trusted artists. Fresh blowout or loose waves work beautifully for a timeless look.

👗

Client Closet Access

I maintain a curated closet of 30+ gowns, wraps, and accessories in sizes XS–2XL — all included in your session at no extra charge.

📅

Timing Is Everything

Book between 28–36 weeks for the most beautiful bump. Too early and the bump is small; too late and comfort decreases. 32 weeks is the sweet spot.

Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

When is the best time to book my Family Portraits session?

I recommend booking between 28–36 weeks of pregnancy, with 32 weeks being the sweet spot. This ensures your bump is beautifully prominent while you're still comfortable and glowing. Please book early — Family Portraits dates fill up quickly, especially in spring and autumn.

Where is your studio located?

My warm, light-filled studio is located in Toronto, Ontario. I also travel to outdoor locations across the GTA — including ravines, gardens, and lakefronts — at no additional charge within 25km of the studio. Exact studio address is shared upon booking.

Can my partner and/or children join the session?

Absolutely — and I encourage it! Partner and sibling portraits are included in all Bare and Heirloom packages. For the Bloom package, family portraits can be added for a small supplement. Children under 3 tend to do best for the first 20–30 minutes, so we start with family shots.

How long until I receive my gallery?

Your private online gallery is delivered within 3–4 weeks of your session. Rush delivery (within 1 week) is available for an additional fee — useful if you're close to your due date. You'll receive a sneak peek on social media within 48 hours of your session.

What if I go into labour before my session date?

Life happens! If baby arrives before your Family Portraits session, we can easily convert your booking into a newborn session instead — at no penalty. I always hold space for flexibility because I understand that pregnancy is beautifully unpredictable.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — I offer interest-free payment plans for all packages. A 30% deposit secures your date, with the remainder due on or before your session day. I want this experience to be accessible without financial stress. Please ask during your consultation.
