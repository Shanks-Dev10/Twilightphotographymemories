ABOUT MY PHOTOGRAPHY JOURNEY..
Hello, amazing people !

I am a professional and certified Maternity, Newborn, and Child Photographer who transitioned from a successful career as a Software Engineer to pursue the artistic path that has always inspired me.

Certified under renowned and ace fashion photographer Karthik Srinivasan at KAPA and further trained by Amrita Samant (MommyShots by Amrita) a pioneer in the maternity and child photography industry.

For me, photography is more than a profession, it is a deeply rooted passion that inspires me to create timeless, meaningful pictures for every family I work with.
