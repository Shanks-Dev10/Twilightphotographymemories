My Story

From code to canvas

My journey into photography began not with a camera, but with a feeling — the overwhelming desire to hold onto time. As a Software Engineer, I was skilled at solving logical problems, but it was the human stories around me that truly captured my heart.

"Photography for me is not just a profession — it is a deeply rooted passion that inspires me to create timeless, meaningful pictures for every family I work with."

I made the bold decision to pursue what had always called to me. I trained under renowned fashion photographer Karthik Srinivasan at KAPA, then further honed my specialty under Amrita Samant (MommyShots by Amrita) — a pioneer in maternity and child photography. Today, I bring that depth of training to every session in Toronto and the GTA.

My Philosophy

Three pillars of every session

Every photograph I create is guided by these core beliefs — a promise I make to every family who trusts me with their most precious moments.

01
🕊️

Safety First

Every newborn, every bump, every little one is handled with the utmost care and gentleness. Your baby's comfort and safety guide every creative decision I make — no pose is ever forced, no moment ever rushed.

02
⏳

Unhurried Patience

The best moments can't be scheduled. I allow sessions to breathe and unfold naturally — sometimes 2 hours becomes 4 — because the perfect shot is worth the wait. I won't stop until we're both truly happy.

03
💎

Timeless Quality

I deliver images that you'll still treasure in 30 years. Every photo is thoughtfully edited, expertly composed, and crafted to hold the emotional truth of that exact moment — not just what you looked like, but how it felt.
